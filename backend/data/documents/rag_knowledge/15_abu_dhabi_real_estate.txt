ABU DHABI vs DUBAI
Abu Dhabi is the capital of the UAE and the wealthiest emirate (oil wealth).
Key differences from Dubai:
- More government-controlled, stable market
- Less foreign buyer activity historically (changing)
- Better quality of life metrics, less traffic
- Stricter regulations, more conservative culture
- Slightly lower yields but more stable
- Foreign ownership allowed in designated Investment Zones only

SAADIYAT ISLAND (CULTURAL DISTRICT)
Concept: Cultural and luxury residential island
Key institutions: Louvre Abu Dhabi, Guggenheim Abu Dhabi (under construction), NYU Abu Dhabi
Property types: Luxury villas, apartments, beachfront
Price range: AED 2,000,000 – 15,000,000+
Areas: Saadiyat Beach, Mamsha Al Saadiyat, Saadiyat Reserve
Why buy: Upcoming Guggenheim will raise property values significantly
Best for: Art and culture lovers, UHNWI, families
Yields: 4-6% gross (lower than Dubai but stable)

YAS ISLAND (ENTERTAINMENT HUB)
Concept: Formula 1, theme parks, entertainment
Key attractions: Ferrari World, Yas Waterworld, Warner Bros, Yas Marina Circuit F1
Property types: Apartments, townhouses, villas
Price range: AED 500,000 – 5,000,000
Developments: Yas Acres (villas), Ansam (apartments), West Yas, Mayan
Why buy: Rental demand from tourism, affordable entry, F1 premium
Yields: 6-8% gross
Best for: Investors, holiday home buyers

AL REEM ISLAND (URBAN APARTMENT MARKET)
Concept: Mixed-use urban island connected to Abu Dhabi CBD
Property types: Studio to 3BR apartments
Price range: AED 500,000 – 3,000,000
Why buy: Connected to Abu Dhabi main island, affordable, government workers demand
Yields: 6-8%
Best for: Working professionals, value investors

JUBAIL ISLAND
Concept: Eco-friendly mangrove island community
Property types: Villas and townhouses
Price range: AED 3,000,000 – 12,000,000
Developer: Aldar Properties
Why buy: Unique natural setting, mangrove views, sustainable design
Best for: Nature-loving families, health-conscious buyers

KEY DEVELOPERS IN ABU DHABI:
- Aldar Properties: Government-linked, most reliable (Yas Acres, Saadiyat Reserve, Al Raha)
- Modon Properties: Government developer (Al Jubail, housing communities)
- Imkan Properties: Premium segment (Makers District, Pixel)
- Eagle Hills: International developer (Reem Island, Saadiyat)

ABU DHABI REGULATIONS:
- DLD equivalent: Abu Dhabi Department of Municipalities and Transport (DMT)
- Transfer fee: 2% of property value (cheaper than Dubai's 4%)
- Agent commission: 2% (similar to Dubai)
- No RERA equivalent — Abu Dhabi Real Estate Centre (ADREC) regulates
- Mortgage: Same UAE Central Bank rules apply
- Golden Visa: Same AED 2M threshold applies in Abu Dhabi

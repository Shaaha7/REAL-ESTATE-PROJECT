UAE PROPERTY PRICE GUIDE — ALL PRICE RANGES IN AED
====================================================

UNDERSTANDING AED PROPERTY VALUES

AED stands for Arab Emirates Dirham. All property prices in the UAE are quoted in AED.
1 USD ≈ 3.67 AED | 1 GBP ≈ 4.60 AED | 1 EUR ≈ 3.95 AED | 1 INR ≈ 0.044 AED

NOTE ON LARGE BUDGETS:
When a client says "AED 20 million" or "20M budget" — this places them in the ultra-premium
villa or penthouse segment. Best matches: Emirates Hills, Palm Jumeirah Fronds, Al Barari,
District One, Jumeirah Bay Island. The client is likely a UHNWI (Ultra High Net Worth Individual).
Always recommend: branded residences, beach villas, golf estate mansions.

PROPERTY NEGOTIATION AND TRANSACTION PROCESS — UAE
====================================================

STEP 1: UNDERSTANDING THE LISTING PRICE

In UAE, listing prices are always in AED (Arab Emirates Dirham).
Prices are typically quoted as:
- Total price: "AED 2.5 million" or "2,500,000 AED" or "AED 2.5M"
- Per square foot: "AED 1,500 per sqft"

Never confuse: AED prices with USD prices (1 USD = 3.67 AED)
Example: A "2 million property" in UAE = AED 2,000,000 = approximately USD 545,000

NEGOTIATION LEVERAGE:
Cash buyers: 5-10% negotiation room on asking price
Mortgage buyers: 3-5% negotiation room
Hot market: 0-2% (seller has leverage when high demand)
Slow market: Up to 15% (buyer has leverage)

WHAT AFFECTS NEGOTIATION:
- How long property has been listed (check listing date on portals)
- Seller's motivation (personal reasons, need cash, relocation)
- Comparable sales in same building/community (check DLD)
- Condition of property vs comparable

CHECKING COMPARABLE SALES (DLD TRANSACTION HISTORY):
Go to: dubaipropertydata.com or Dubai REST app or DLD website
Search: Property address or community name
Filter: Last 3-6 months of transactions
This gives you ACTUAL sold prices, not asking prices

MAKING AN OFFER:
Offer is typically verbal first through your agent
Then confirmed in writing
With 10% deposit cheque (held by agent until MOU signed)

MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU / FORM F):
Official contract between buyer and seller:
- Standard form: RERA Form F
- Price, deposit paid, completion date
- What is included (furniture, parking, storage)
- Who pays NOC fee
- Penalties for default (typically 10% of purchase price)
- Time limit to complete (typically 30-60 days for cash, 90 days for mortgage)

DEPOSIT:
Standard: 10% of purchase price
Held by: Licensed real estate agent (in their client account)
If buyer defaults: Seller keeps 10% deposit
If seller defaults: Buyer gets 10% deposit back + can claim damages

PRE-TRANSFER CHECKLIST:
□ NOC obtained from developer (5-15 working days)
□ Outstanding service charges paid by seller
□ Mortgage offer letter received (if financed)
□ Both parties arranged power of attorney (if not attending in person)
□ Manager's cheques prepared for DLD transfer day:
  - Cheque to seller for balance payment
  - Cheque to DLD for transfer fee (4%)
  - Cheque to agency (commission)
  - Cheque for DLD admin fees

TRANSFER DAY AT DLD (DUBAI LAND DEPARTMENT):
Location: Various DLD offices across Dubai (Deira main office or satellite offices)
Timing: Book appointment in advance (DLD website)
Duration: 30-90 minutes depending on property and payment type
Required at DLD:
- Seller (or POA holder) with original passport
- Buyer (or POA holder) with original passport
- Original title deed
- NOC from developer
- All manager's cheques
Result: New title deed issued in buyer's name on same day or within 1-3 days

PROTECTING YOURSELF:
1. Always use a RERA-licensed agent (verify on Dubai REST app)
2. Never pay cash directly to seller — always through official channels
3. Verify seller is the actual title deed holder (check DLD, ask to see title deed)
4. Do not sign MOU without reading carefully (or get lawyer to review)
5. Get independent mortgage advice (not just from seller's agent)
6. Check outstanding service charges before completion (seller must clear all)
7. For AED 5M+ transactions: consider hiring a UAE property lawyer (AED 5,000-15,000)

TIMELINE EXPECTATIONS:
Simple cash purchase: 2-4 weeks from offer to title deed
Standard cash purchase: 4-6 weeks
Mortgage purchase: 6-10 weeks
Off-plan transfer: 2-4 weeks from NOC receipt

COMMON ISSUES AND SOLUTIONS:
Issue: NOC delayed → Solution: Follow up directly with developer, agent to escalate
Issue: Bank valuation lower than agreed price → Solution: Renegotiate with seller or increase down payment
Issue: Seller trying to back out → Solution: MOU penalty clause protects you (they owe you 10% + potential damages)
Issue: Unexpected service charge arrears → Solution: Seller must clear before transfer, withhold from final payment

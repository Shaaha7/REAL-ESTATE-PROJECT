ULTRA-LUXURY PROPERTIES IN UAE — AED 20 MILLION AND ABOVE
==========================================================

THE AED 20 MILLION+ MARKET — WHO IS BUYING?

Properties priced at AED 20,000,000 (twenty million dirhams) and above represent
the apex of UAE real estate. Buyers in this segment include:
- Global billionaires and centimillionaires relocating to UAE
- Royalty and government officials from GCC, India, Russia, UK, USA
- Tech entrepreneurs and hedge fund managers
- Family offices seeking stable wealth preservation
- Celebrities and sports stars

WHY UAE FOR AED 20M+ PROPERTIES?
1. Zero personal income tax (save 40-50% vs UK/EU)
2. Political stability and security
3. World-class infrastructure and lifestyle
4. Year-round sunshine (avg 3,500 sunshine hours/year)
5. Central location: 4hrs from India, 7hrs from UK, 8hrs from Singapore
6. No capital gains tax, no inheritance tax
7. Golden Visa for AED 2M+ (permanent residency pathway)

PROPERTY CATEGORIES AT AED 20M+

CATEGORY 1: PALM JUMEIRAH SIGNATURE VILLAS (AED 20M – 50M)
- 6-7 bedroom frond villas
- Private beach (typically 20-30 meters frontage)
- Private pool and garden
- Basement with gym, cinema, staff quarters
- Sea views of Atlantis and Dubai Marina skyline
- Size: 8,000 – 15,000 sqft BUA
- Plot size: 12,000 – 20,000 sqft
- Annual service charge: AED 150,000 – 300,000
- Estimated rental (yearly): AED 800,000 – 2,000,000
- Typical sellers: Upgrading to Jumeirah Bay or Al Barari
- Transaction examples: AED 22M (6BR, 10,000sqft), AED 35M (7BR, waterfront)

CATEGORY 2: EMIRATES HILLS MANSIONS (AED 20M – 80M)
- Completely bespoke, no two are alike
- Overlooking Montgomerie Golf Course (18-hole championship course)
- 7-10 bedrooms, multiple staff quarters
- Private pool, tennis court, basketball court possible
- High ceilings, grand entrance halls
- Size: 12,000 – 30,000 sqft BUA
- Plot size: 20,000 – 50,000 sqft
- Annual service charge: AED 200,000 – 500,000
- Notable features: Some have private cinema, wine cellar, wellness spa
- Who buys: Old money, industrialists, government officials
- Transaction examples: AED 25M (standard mansion), AED 55M (mega-mansion)

CATEGORY 3: JUMEIRAH BAY ISLAND — BULGARI BRANDED (AED 25M – 100M)
- Only island development with Bulgari resort and marina
- Limited to 16 ocean mansions (most exclusive in Dubai)
- Branded interiors with Bulgari design standards
- Access to Bulgari Hotel, spa, restaurants and private marina
- Size: 10,000 – 25,000 sqft
- Sea frontage and panoramic views of Dubai Marina skyline
- Extremely limited supply — when available sells immediately
- Transaction record: AED 96M (waterfront mansion)

CATEGORY 4: AL BARARI ESTATES (AED 15M – 45M)
- Ecological community, 60% green space and botanical gardens
- 3km of waterways and gardens
- Carbon-neutral design, solar-powered
- 4-7 bedroom villas
- Size: 8,000 – 20,000 sqft BUA
- Annual service charge: AED 120,000 – 250,000
- Who buys: Environmentally conscious UHNWI, families
- Development: The Nest, The Residences, The Reserve

CATEGORY 5: DISTRICT ONE — MOHAMMED BIN RASHID CITY (AED 15M – 30M)
- Crystal lagoon community — world's largest man-made lagoon
- 7km of white sandy beach (private within community)
- 3-6 bedroom villas and mansions
- Developer: Meydan Group
- Size: 6,000 – 15,000 sqft BUA
- 5km cycling/running track, waterpark, beach club
- Central location — 10 min to Downtown Dubai

CATEGORY 6: BRANDED RESIDENCES (AED 15M – 100M+)
The fastest growing segment in UAE luxury:
- Four Seasons DIFC: AED 15M – 45M
- Six Senses Residences The Palm: AED 20M – 50M
- Atlantis The Royal Residences: AED 15M – 100M+
- Armani Residences: AED 10M – 30M (Downtown Burj Khalifa)
- W Residences Dubai: AED 5M – 20M
- Address Residences (Emaar): AED 5M – 25M

WHY BRANDED RESIDENCES?
- Hotel services (concierge, housekeeping, room service) in your home
- Strong brand recognition drives resale value
- Professional management for rental when not in use
- Global network of buyers familiar with the brand

FINANCING ULTRA-LUXURY PROPERTIES
Most AED 20M+ transactions are CASH:
- Banks do mortgage up to AED 20M (but rarely above)
- High-net-worth clients prefer no debt
- Private banking: Julius Baer, Lombard Odier, UBS offer bespoke financing
- Developer payment plans on off-plan ultra-luxury: Sometimes 20/80 (20% now, 80% on delivery)

AED 20M BUDGET — PROPAI RECOMMENDATION:
With a budget of AED 20 million (twenty million dirhams), PropAI recommends:
1. Palm Jumeirah Frond Villa: 5-6BR, private beach, 8,000 sqft — ideal value
2. Emirates Hills Mansion: If prestige and golf are priority
3. District One Villa: If family and beach lifestyle is priority
4. Branded Residence (Six Senses, Atlantis): If hotel services and brand matter

NEGOTIATION AT AED 20M+ LEVEL:
- Cash buyers have 5-10% negotiation leverage
- Market is international — competition from global buyers
- Always check DLD transaction history for comparable sales
- RERA index does not apply above certain thresholds — open market pricing
- Legal due diligence (NOC, title search, developer track record) is critical

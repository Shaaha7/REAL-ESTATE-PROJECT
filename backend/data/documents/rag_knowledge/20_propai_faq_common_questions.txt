PROPAI REAL ESTATE AI — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
====================================================

Q: What does "AED 20 million" or "AED 20M" mean?
A: AED is the currency of the UAE (Arab Emirates Dirham). 20 million AED (20,000,000 AED) 
is approximately USD 5.4 million or GBP 4.3 million. This is an ultra-premium budget in Dubai 
that would buy: a Palm Jumeirah signature villa, an Emirates Hills mansion, a District One 
lakefront villa, or a high-floor penthouse in Downtown Dubai or Business Bay.

Q: What is the difference between freehold and leasehold?
A: Freehold = you own the property and the land forever, no time limit.
Leasehold = you have the right to use/occupy for a fixed period (typically 99 years), 
land reverts to owner after. Foreign nationals can only buy freehold in designated zones.

Q: Can foreigners buy property in Dubai?
A: Yes! Foreign nationals (any nationality) can buy freehold property in over 60 designated 
areas including Dubai Marina, Downtown Dubai, Palm Jumeirah, JLT, Business Bay, JVC, 
Dubai Hills Estate, Arabian Ranches, and many more. There are no restrictions on nationality.

Q: How much does it cost to buy a property in Dubai?
A: Add 7-8% to the purchase price for all costs:
4% DLD transfer fee + 2% agent commission + ~2% admin fees/mortgage costs
Example: AED 2,000,000 property = AED 2,160,000 total (AED 160,000 costs)
Example: AED 10,000,000 property = AED 10,800,000 total (AED 800,000 costs)

Q: What is the minimum budget to buy in Dubai?
A: Studios start from AED 250,000-350,000 (International City, Discovery Gardens).
For a decent 1BR apartment: Budget AED 500,000 minimum in JVC or Dubai Silicon Oasis.
For a villa: Budget AED 2,500,000 minimum for a 3BR townhouse in JVC.

Q: What is the Golden Visa and how does property qualify?
A: The UAE Golden Visa gives you 10-year renewable residency.
Property route: Buy a completed property worth AED 2,000,000+ (fully paid, not mortgaged).
Benefits: Live, work, study in UAE without a national sponsor. Sponsor family members.
Application: Through GDRFA (Dubai) or ICA (Federal). Takes 2-4 weeks. Costs approx. AED 5,000.

Q: What is an off-plan property?
A: Off-plan = property purchased before construction is complete, based on floor plans.
Benefits: Lower price (10-30% below market), flexible payment plans (pay during construction),
potential capital gains by handover date.
Risk: Construction delays, developer reliability, market changes.

Q: Is Dubai a good place to invest in real estate?
A: Strong case FOR investing:
✓ Zero income tax on rental income
✓ Zero capital gains tax when you sell
✓ High gross yields (5-9% for apartments)
✓ Stable currency (AED pegged to USD since 1997)
✓ Strong rental demand (3.5M+ population, growing)
✓ Growing global status as financial and tourism hub
✓ Golden Visa pathway for AED 2M+ buyers
Consider: Market is relatively young, some volatility, service charges reduce net yield.

Q: How long does it take to buy a property in Dubai?
A: Cash purchase: 3-6 weeks from offer to title deed.
Mortgage purchase: 6-10 weeks (bank approval takes time).
Off-plan: Immediate on launch, wait for construction (1-4 years).

Q: What is DLD?
A: DLD = Dubai Land Department. The government authority that registers all property 
transactions, issues title deeds, and regulates real estate in Dubai. 
All legal property transfers MUST go through DLD. DLD charges the 4% transfer fee.

Q: What is RERA?
A: RERA = Real Estate Regulatory Agency. The regulatory arm of DLD.
RERA licenses all real estate agents and brokers, regulates rentals (RERA index),
oversees off-plan project escrow accounts, and handles developer disputes.
Always verify your agent's RERA number on the Dubai REST app.

Q: What is a service charge and do I have to pay it?
A: Yes — service charges are mandatory annual fees paid by all owners in jointly owned 
properties (apartments, townhouses in communities). They cover: building maintenance, 
security, pools, gyms, landscaping, elevators. Rates: AED 8-30 per sqft per year.
Non-payment can result in: penalties, blacklisting, inability to sell.

Q: What happens if I don't pay service charges?
A: The Owners Association can: charge interest penalties, refer to RERA for enforcement,
place a lien on your property, and you cannot transfer/sell the property until all 
outstanding service charges are cleared.

UAE PROPERTY — ALL TAXES AND COSTS EXPLAINED
=============================================

THE BASICS: UAE HAS ZERO PERSONAL INCOME TAX

UAE is famous globally for having no personal income tax:
- No income tax on salary ✓
- No capital gains tax on property profits ✓
- No inheritance tax ✓
- No wealth tax ✓
- No annual property tax ✓

This is WHY property in UAE offers superior net returns vs UK, USA, Australia.

UK comparison example:
UK property: 7% gross yield - 40% income tax - CGT = ~3.5% net
UAE property: 7% gross yield - 0% income tax - 0% CGT = 6.5% net (after costs)

ONE-TIME COSTS WHEN BUYING (BUYER PAYS):

1. DLD TRANSFER FEE: 4% of purchase price
   Example: AED 2,000,000 property → AED 80,000 DLD fee
   Example: AED 5,000,000 property → AED 200,000 DLD fee
   Example: AED 10,000,000 property → AED 400,000 DLD fee
   Example: AED 20,000,000 property → AED 800,000 DLD fee
   Payment: To Dubai Land Department on transfer day (cheque or bank transfer)

2. DLD ADMIN / KNOWLEDGE FEE:
   Properties under AED 500,000: AED 4,000
   Properties above AED 500,000: AED 4,580 (AED 4,000 + AED 580)

3. AGENT COMMISSION (BUYER): Typically 2% of purchase price
   Example: AED 2,000,000 → AED 40,000 to buyer's agent
   Note: In Dubai, buyer typically pays their own agent 2%

4. TITLE DEED AND REGISTRATION:
   Title deed fee: AED 250
   Map/plot registration: AED 250 (land) or AED 100 (apartment)
   Typing charges: AED 1,040 – 4,000

5. MORTGAGE COSTS (if financed):
   Bank arrangement fee: 0.5-1% of loan amount
   Property valuation: AED 2,500 – 3,500 (independent valuer)
   Mortgage registration (DLD): 0.25% of loan amount
   Mortgage protection insurance: Approximately 0.3-0.5% of loan annually
   Life insurance (many banks require): Varies

6. NOC FEE (if applicable): AED 500 – 5,000 (developer-dependent)

TOTAL BUYING COSTS SUMMARY:
Budget for: 7-8% of purchase price for all closing costs
Example: AED 2M purchase → ~AED 160,000 total costs
Example: AED 5M purchase → ~AED 400,000 total costs
Example: AED 10M purchase → ~AED 800,000 total costs
Example: AED 20M purchase → ~AED 1,600,000 total costs

SELLER PAYS:
- Agent commission: 2% (typically seller pays listing agent)
- NOC fee from developer: AED 500-5,000
- Outstanding service charges must be cleared
- Capital gains: ZERO (no CGT in UAE)
- Seller does NOT pay DLD transfer fee (buyer does)

ANNUAL ONGOING COSTS (PROPERTY OWNER):
1. Service charges: AED 8-30/sqft/year (see document 09 for details)
2. DEWA (electricity and water): Typically AED 500-3,000/month depending on property size
3. Cooling/chiller: AED 300-1,500/month (unless chiller-free building)
4. Building insurance: AED 1,000-5,000/year (your personal contents)
5. Minor maintenance: 0.5-1% of property value annually
6. Property management: 5-10% of annual rent (if renting out)

CORPORATE OWNERSHIP COSTS:
Additional to above:
- UAE company licensing: AED 15,000-30,000/year
- Accounting/audit: AED 5,000-15,000/year
- No additional property tax (same as individual ownership)

VAT IN UAE REAL ESTATE:
VAT was introduced in UAE in 2018 at 5%
Property VAT rules:
- First sale of residential property by developer: ZERO RATED (0% VAT)
- Resale of residential property: EXEMPT (no VAT)
- Commercial property (offices, retail): 5% VAT on sale and rent
- Short-term residential rental: 5% VAT on rent (holiday homes)
- Long-term residential rental (>6 months): EXEMPT from VAT

IMPORTANT: Residential property transactions are generally VAT-free.
